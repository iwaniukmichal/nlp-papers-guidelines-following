**The 3 most important problems**

1. **2022 ARR checklist status.** All 21 rows from ARR-RNLP-2022 are coded `checklist_question`, but no 2022 source says the checklist must be answered. ARR-CFP-2022 says authors "should upload" it, and the RNLP page says "We expect authors to show … by filling in the checklist". "must complete" first appears in ARR-CFP-2024. For 2023 the ACL 2023 AI policy calls it "the mandatory Responsible NLP Checklist", so I accepted 2023. For 2022 the status is genuinely between `checklist_question` and `recommended`. This affects the 2022 ARR column of any compliance-vs-requirement analysis.
2. **Validation performance is missed for ARR (2022–2026).** C3 guidance says "When reporting a result on a test set, be sure to report a result of the same model on the validation set (if available)". This is the same kind of evidence used to code DATA_PREPROCESSING from C2 guidance. Even so, VALIDATION_PERFORMANCE is `none` for 2024–26 and has no arr row in 2022–23. The same pattern explains several smaller misses: the Ethics FAQ (2021–22) recommends things coded as `none` (RISKS, ARTIFACT_DOCUMENTATION, COMPUTE_BUDGET in 2022).
3. **Route scoping errors.** (a) 2021: evidence rows use `route=direct`, but `none` rows use `route=all`. That asserts ARR-pilot papers had no requirements, which cannot be known because no ARR document exists for 2021. Those rows should be split into direct `none` and arr `unverified`. (b) 2022–23: the EMNLP CFP's supplementary-material clause is coded `route=all`, but ARR commitments "cannot be modified" and follow ARR's own clause. It should be `direct`. (c) 2022–23: it is unclear whether EMNLP's ethics sanction and Ethics FAQ cover ARR commitments (marked UNSURE).

**Cross-year issues not tied to a single row**

- `tracks` uses three vocabularies: `main` (2019), `main+findings` (2020–21) and `all_submissions` (2022+). 2022–23 CFPs never mention Findings, so `all_submissions` is defensible, but the codebook should define all three.
- `condition` is empty for checklist items that sit under conditional headings ("For all experiments with hyperparameter search", "For all datasets used" in 2020–23; ARR sections B/C/D "Did you use or create scientific artifacts?" etc.). It does not change the status (N/A is allowed), but the D-check asked for it.
- 2023: only 4 reqs have an `unverified` row for the missing EMNLP 2023 Ethics FAQ. The 2021–22 FAQ also covered RISKS, COMPUTE_BUDGET, ARTIFACT_DOCUMENTATION and ARTIFACT_LICENSE.
- EMNLP2025-CFP and EMNLP2026-CFP require authors to follow "ACL’s Publication Ethics Policy" and "ACL’s Disclosure Policy". Neither is in the corpus, so any reporting duties in them are unassessed.
- `where_reported=checklist` for ARR items is slightly misleading. The checklist asks for the paper section where the information is ("indicate the section(s)"), so the information itself lives in the paper or appendix.

## Dictionary issues (task G)

1. **COMPUTE_BUDGET merges different quantities:** 2020–22 "Average runtime for each approach / model" (per model, possibly inference), "estimated energy cost", and ARR C1 "total computational budget (e.g., GPU hours)" covering *all* experiments. A paper can satisfy one and not the other. Split it, or record which variant is required.
2. **RESULT_STATISTICS merges two statistics:** 2020's "Expected validation performance … as a function of the number of hyperparameter trials" (a hyperparameter-search statistic) and variance over runs or seeds, error bars and max/mean (2021 "Summary statistics", ARR C3). It also absorbs "The exact number of training and evaluation runs".
3. **C2 is mapped twice.** HYPERPARAMETERS = C2, and DATA_PREPROCESSING = C2 (guidance). HYPERPARAMETERS itself merges four 2020–21 items (bounds, best configuration, number of trials, selection method) plus "model selection" from C2 guidance. Coding a paper as compliant on C2 says nothing about which of these it reports.
4. **DATA_STATISTICS vs ARTIFACT_DOCUMENTATION:** the 2021–23 item "Relevant details such as languages, and number of examples and label distributions" mixes B6 statistics with B5 documentation (languages).
5. **DATA_COLLECTION_DESCRIPTION vs ANNOTATOR_INSTRUCTIONS:** the 2020–23 item lists "instructions to annotators" as part of the collection description. The boundary with D1 ("full text of instructions") needs a rule.
6. **ETHICS_DISCUSSION vs RISKS:** ETHICS_DISCUSSION's definition includes "broader impact statement", and ARR's ethics paragraph asks for "a discussion of broader impacts". Both overlap with RISKS (A2: negative societal impact). One paper section can satisfy both.
7. **ARTIFACT_INTENDED_USE** merges two things: consistency of using existing artifacts, and specifying the intended use of created artifacts. The 2021–22 FAQ's "Intended use" of a *technology* is a third concept and belongs under RISKS.
8. **VALIDATION_PERFORMANCE** should map to "C3 (guidance)". **EVALUATION_METRICS** partly maps to C4 (evaluation packages such as ROUGE).
9. **AI_DISCLOSURE_IN_PAPER** merges writing and coding assistance. For coding, ARR allows README files, so where_reported is not always the paper.
10. **LIMITATIONS_SECTION** in 2025–26 also has a content restriction (no new results). That restriction is not a reporting requirement and should not be a separate evidence row.
11. **In scope but missing from the dictionary:** comparison with and citation of related or contemporaneous work (2020–23 CFPs); justification of identity categories (2021–22 FAQ); discussion of carbon mitigation (2021–22 FAQ); data-quality risks (2021–22 FAQ); disclosure of code-assistant use in READMEs (2023–26).

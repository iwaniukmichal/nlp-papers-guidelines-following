# Rubric Item Selection Log (Stage 3)

This document records the Stage 3 item selection decisions following the bottom-up methodology in [docs/methodology/rubric-development.md](../../docs/methodology/rubric-development.md). Starting from the verified inventory of **34 atomic reporting requirements** ([config/policy_register/requirement_dictionary.csv](../policy_register/requirement_dictionary.csv) and [config/policy_register/inventory_by_year.csv](../policy_register/inventory_by_year.csv)), we evaluate every candidate against six selection criteria and document why it is **Selected** (into the human+LLM rubric `I1`–`I6` or as an automatic indicator `A1`) or **Excluded**.

---

## 1. Selection Criteria

Each of the 34 requirements in `requirement_dictionary.csv` is evaluated on six criteria (`Pass` / `Partial` / `Fail`):

1. **C1 — Topic coverage (`Area`):** Belongs to one of the five core areas of the study (`limitations`, `reproducibility`, `responsible_research`, `model_data_documentation`, `ai_tools`). The final rubric must cover all five areas.
2. **C2 — Policy variation (`Var.`):** Policy status changes at least once across EMNLP 2020–2026 (providing an identifiable transition for **RQ2 / H1**), or serves as a deliberate baseline/era indicator (**RQ1 / RQ3**).
3. **C3 — Observable in paper (`Obs.`):** Can be evaluated directly from the published paper and ordinary appendices (`where_reported = paper / appendix`), without relying on submission checklists (stripped to avoid the EMNLP 2025+ availability confound) or external `.zip` supplementary archives.
4. **C4 — Reliably codable (`Cod.`):** Two trained human annotators and an LLM evaluator can judge presence and adequacy with high agreement using a written operational definition, without requiring deep domain-specific re-derivation of the paper's math or experiments.
5. **C5 — Applicability decidable (`App.`):** Whether the requirement applies to a given paper (`practice_applies`) can be determined reliably from the paper text alone (e.g., whether the paper runs computational experiments or uses/releases artifacts, as opposed to unknowable internal workflows).
6. **C6 — Annotation workload (`Work.`):** Keeps total manual double-annotation effort feasible across the 14 pilot, 70 development, and 140 held-out test papers ($\le 6$ manual items per paper).

## 4. Methodological Notes on Combined Indicators (`I3` and `I4`)

In accordance with the proposal (*"Fixed presence indicators measure adoption before and after mandates; changed definitions require a common indicator or separate series"*):

1. **`I3_ethical_risks` (`ETHICS_DISCUSSION` + `RISKS`):**
   - For **adoption analysis (RQ2 / RQ3)** across all papers, `I3` records whether a paper explicitly discusses ethical considerations, societal impacts, or potential risks/harms of the work (matching ARR `A2` `RISKS`, which applies to all papers from 2024, and `ETHICS_DISCUSSION`).
   - For **compliance analysis (RQ1)**, `policy_applies` is `True` throughout 2020–2026 under `ETHICS_DISCUSSION` (`conditional_mandatory`), and `practice_applies` is restricted to papers raising concrete ethical concerns (working with human subjects/annotators, PII/sensitive demographics, or high-risk/harm tasks).

2. **`I4_computational_budget` (`COMPUTE_INFRASTRUCTURE` + `COMPUTE_RUNTIME` / `COMPUTE_BUDGET_TOTAL`):**
   - Because the 2020–2023 Reproducibility Checklist asked for `COMPUTE_INFRASTRUCTURE` + `COMPUTE_RUNTIME` (*"average runtime for each approach, or estimated energy cost"*) whereas the 2022–2026 ARR Checklist `C1` asks for `COMPUTE_INFRASTRUCTURE` + `COMPUTE_BUDGET_TOTAL` (*"total computational budget, e.g., GPU hours"*), we define `I4` as a **common longitudinal indicator**:
     - `adequately_reported`: The paper reports **both** the computing hardware/infrastructure used (e.g., GPU/TPU/CPU model or count) **and** a quantitative measure of compute time, budget, or energy (per-run runtime, wall-clock training time, GPU-hours, FLOPs, or kWh/CO$_2$).
     - `explicitly_reported_inadequate`: The paper mentions hardware without any duration/budget (or vice versa), or only gives training epochs/batch size without hardware or time.
     - `not_reported`: Neither hardware nor compute time/budget is reported.

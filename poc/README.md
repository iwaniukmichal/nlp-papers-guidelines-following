# EMNLP 2020–2026 Reporting Compliance Audit — Proof of Concept (PoC)

This directory contains the complete pipeline and results for the **Proof of Concept (PoC)** conducted on a stratified pilot sample of **14 papers (2 per year across 2020–2026: 1 Main + 1 Findings, `seed = 42`)**.

## Directory Structure (`poc/`)

### 1. Policy Register and Deductive Rubric
- [`policy_register.json`](policy_register.json) — Versioned historical policy register for **EMNLP 2020–2026** and **ACL Rolling Review (ARR)**, including document URLs, effective dates, tracks covered, and redistribution terms.
- [`rubric.json`](rubric.json) — Formalized 6-item deductive coding rubric (`I1_limitations_section`, `I2_specific_limitation`, `I3_ethical_risks`, `I4_computational_budget`, `I5_asset_licensing`, `I6_ai_assistance`) separating:
  1. `policy_applies` (whether a mandatory obligation applied in the publication year),
  2. `practice_applies` (whether the reporting practice applies to the paper's research methodology),
  3. `evidence_status` (4 mutually exclusive states: `not_applicable`, `not_reported`, `explicitly_reported_inadequate`, `adequately_reported`),
  4. `derived_compliance` (`Compliant`, `Non-compliant`, `Not applicable`, `Uncertain`).

### 2. Pilot Data and Evaluation Outputs (`poc/data/`)
- [`data/corpus_counts_by_year_track.json`](data/corpus_counts_by_year_track.json) — Exact counts of eligible EMNLP 2020–2026 papers by year and track (**16,618 eligible papers in total**: 9,254 Main + 7,364 Findings).
- [`data/emnlp_2020_2026_eligible_manifest.csv`](data/emnlp_2020_2026_eligible_manifest.csv) — Complete manifest of all 16,618 eligible papers extracted from the official ACL Anthology XML records.
- [`data/pilot_14_sample.csv`](data/pilot_14_sample.csv) / [`data/pilot_14_sample.json`](data/pilot_14_sample.json) — Stratified pilot sample of 14 papers (2 per year) with recorded inclusion probabilities and random seed.
- [`data/poc_baseline_evaluations.csv`](data/poc_baseline_evaluations.csv) — 84 evaluations (14 papers × 6 items) generated by the deterministic rule-based baseline evaluator (`rule_baseline_v1`).
- [`data/poc_llm_evaluations.csv`](data/poc_llm_evaluations.csv) / [`data/poc_llm_evaluations.json`](data/poc_llm_evaluations.json) — 84 evaluations generated by `gemini-2.5-flash`, including verbatim evidence quotes (`evidence_quote`), automatic substring match verification (`quote_verified_in_text`), page/section location (`location`), and rationale (`justification`).
- [`data/poc_llm_usage_stats.json`](data/poc_llm_usage_stats.json) — Per-paper token consumption and latency statistics.
- [`data/poc_summary_report.json`](data/poc_summary_report.json) — Aggregated pilot summary (class distributions, pre-2022 vs. post-2022 comparisons, rule-vs-LLM agreement, and full-corpus cost projections).
- [`data/human_annotation_sheet_pilot14.csv`](data/human_annotation_sheet_pilot14.csv) — **Blind double-annotation sheet** (168 rows = 14 papers × 6 items × 2 independent human annotators; 7 papers assigned per annotator pair).

### 3. Pipeline Scripts (`poc/*.py`)
1. `python poc/collect_emnlp_poc.py` — Downloads ACL Anthology XML metadata, builds the 16,618-paper manifest, and downloads the 14 pilot PDFs into `poc/data/pdfs/`.
2. `python poc/evaluate_poc.py` — Parses PDFs into `poc/data/parsed/` (removing author email PII and appended EMNLP 2025+ checklists), generates the blind human annotation sheet, and runs the rule-based baseline evaluator.
3. `python poc/run_llm_evaluator.py` — Runs the Gemini LLM evaluator (`GEMINI_API_KEY` read strictly from the environment variable).
4. `python poc/summarize_poc_results.py` — Generates aggregated pilot statistics and cost estimates in `poc/data/poc_summary_report.json`.
5. `python poc/compute_iaa_and_validation.py` — Once human annotators complete the `evidence_status_human` column in `data/human_annotation_sheet_pilot14.csv`, computes per-item **Cohen's $\kappa$** (human–human agreement) and **Macro-F1** (LLM vs. human reference).
